# Density-functional calculations

This directory contains the VASP calculations of anion orderings in
NbO<sub>2</sub>F, in four sets that serve different purposes, and a fifth set
for TaO<sub>2</sub>F.

- `3x3x3/`: 92 configurations in the 3&times;3&times;3 supercell, spanning random
  anion placements, period-three orderings with random inter-chain phase, and the
  twelve enumerated cis + OOF orbit representatives. These establish that the two
  ordering rules are energetically active, and are the set from which the
  order-parameter analysis is built.
- `6x6x6/`: 96 configurations in the 6&times;6&times;6 supercell, the training set
  for the cluster expansion. Directory names record how each was obtained:
  `P3121_tiled` is the tiled ground state, `buss_*` and `config_*` were proposed
  by the active-learning search described in
  `docs/cluster-expansion-workflow.md`.
- `3x3x3_r2scan/`: r2SCAN full geometry optimisations of the twelve orbit
  representatives of `3x3x3/`, with orbit 11 in two settings. Each starts from
  the relaxed PBEsol structure of the `3x3x3/` configuration of the same name,
  with the same *k*-point mesh and numerical settings, so the two sets differ
  only in the exchange&ndash;correlation functional. They test whether the
  ordering of the orbits depends on the functional.
- `3x3x6/`: Every class of achiral arrangement in the 3&times;3&times;6
  supercell that obeys both ordering rules, fifteen in all; the four
  lowest-energy arrangements with cis coordination at every cation that break
  the chain ordering; and the homochiral *P*3<sub>1</sub>21 ordering for
  reference.
  - `stack_<s>` are stackings of the *P*3<sub>1</sub>21 ordering: in each (001)
    plane of cations the ordering keeps its labelling (0) or takes that of its
    mirror image, *P*3<sub>2</sub>21 (1), so a change between adjacent planes is
    a twin boundary between the two hands. `stack_000000` is the homochiral
    ordering. The other three are the achiral classes built only from
    *P*3<sub>1</sub>21 and orbit-10 blocks, generated by
    `methods/dft/build_stackings.py`.
  - `achiral_NN` are the other twelve achiral classes, numbered in order of
    increasing cluster-expansion energy. None contains a *P*3<sub>1</sub>21
    block. They are enumerated, and their starting structures generated, by
    `methods/dft/enumerate_3x3x6.py`.
  - `cis_nonoof_NN` have exact cis coordination at every cation but not
    period-three OOF order on every chain: two z-chains carry their two
    fluorides one site apart (O&ndash;O&ndash;F&ndash;O&ndash;F&ndash;O). They are
    the four classes of such arrangements on this cell with the lowest
    cluster-expansion energy, numbered in that order.

  The *k*-point mesh folds onto the Monkhorst&ndash;Pack points of the `3x3x3/`
  set.
- `TaO2F_3x3x3/`: 29 configurations of TaO<sub>2</sub>F in the
  3&times;3&times;3 supercell. Each has the O/F arrangement of the `3x3x3/`
  configuration of the same name, with Ta in place of Nb, and the same *k*-point
  mesh and numerical settings. They are the twelve orbit representatives, with
  orbit 11 in two settings, and eight each of the random anion placements and
  the period-three orderings with random inter-chain phase, chosen at evenly
  spaced quantiles of their NbO<sub>2</sub>F energies. They test whether the
  ordering rules and the ordering of the orbits carry over to a second compound.

The `3x3x3/` and `6x6x6/` sets use different *k*-point meshes and their energies
are not directly comparable with each other.

Each configuration directory holds:

- `INCAR`, `KPOINTS`: The calculation parameters.
- `POSCAR.init`: The ideal, undistorted starting structure &mdash; the anion
  ordering itself, and the structure the order parameters are computed from.
- `POSCAR`: The relaxed structure. `3x3x3_r2scan/` has no `POSCAR.init`; the
  ideal structure is that of the configuration of the same name in `3x3x3/`.
- `OUTCAR`: The calculation output, from which the energies are read.
- `vaspmeta.yaml`: Provenance notes for the calculation.
- `POTCAR.spec`: The pseudopotentials used, one per species, with their hashes.
  The `POTCAR` files themselves are licensed by VASP and are not redistributed;
  this records which to obtain in order to repeat the calculations.
